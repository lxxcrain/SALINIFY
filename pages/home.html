Kapampangan Info
✖

Kapampangan

  • 🗣️ Austronesian language spoken in the Philippines
  • 💬 Known for rich and expressive vocabulary
  • 🎧 Has a smooth and melodic sound
  • ✍️ Uses unique words not commonly found
  • 📚 Often used in storytelling and media
  • 🔤 Words may sound different from Tagalog
  • 💡 One of the older native languages

Ilocano

  • 🗣️ Austronesian language widely used
  • 💬 Known for direct and practical expressions
  • 🎧 Has a strong and firm pronunciation
  • ✍️ Shares similarities with northern dialects
  • 📚 Used in daily communication
  • 🔤 Easier to recognize for beginners
  • 💡 One of the most widely spoken
